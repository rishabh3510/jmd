About Us

Building Trust,
Delivering Excellence

JMD Maha Mewa Group's Pvt. Ltd. is a professional construction and government contracting company based in Gurugram, Haryana. Established in 2019, the company has grown into a trusted government contractor and officially expanded into a Private Limited entity in 2026, under the able leadership of its directors, Mr. Manish Shukla and Mrs. Aarti Shukla.

We specialize in end-to-end civil, structural, electrical, plumbing, mechanical and HVAC contracting — from site development and foundation work to finishing, testing and final handover. Our team of engineers and skilled professionals ensures every project meets the highest standards of quality, safety and regulatory compliance.
